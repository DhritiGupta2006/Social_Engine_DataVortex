## 9. Task Selection Decision

**Primary task — Sentiment Classification (`sentiment_label`, 3-class): CONFIRMED.**
The column is perfectly balanced (3,000 per class), content-correlated upon manual inspection, has a low majority baseline of 33.33% (making any improvement meaningful), and no label noise in duplicate groups. It is the clear, low-risk primary target for the full pipeline.

**Secondary task — Topic Classification (`topic_category`, 4-class): CONDITIONALLY CONFIRMED AS STRETCH TARGET.**
The column is severely imbalanced (86.1% majority class), labels do not visibly correlate with post content in manual spot-checks, and the TF-IDF + LR diagnostic achieves macro-F1 of 0.3768 vs. the majority baseline of 0.2314 (a margin >5pp), suggesting some signal exists in the text for distinguishing topic categories. However, given the extreme imbalance and the spot-checked evidence that labels do not visibly correlate with content, this task remains a stretch objective requiring careful evaluation. This task is only attempted after the primary pipeline (Phases 2–8) is fully complete, and must be framed as a class-imbalance / weak-signal case study with explicit baseline comparisons.

**NER — OUT OF SCOPE.**
The dataset contains no entity span annotations; attempting NER would require either an off-the-shelf tagger with no ground truth to evaluate against (unverifiable claims) or fabricated labels — both violate the competition's 'no unsupported claims / no fabricated data' rules.
